---

## What I Learned
- **Security Analyst:** Monitors, investigates, and responds to suspicious
activity. Works primarily within a SOC (Security Operations Center).
- **Security Engineer:** Designs and maintains the systems and infrastructure
that protect an organization.
- **Penetration Tester:** Simulates real attacks to find vulnerabilities before
malicious actors do.

---

## Reflection
